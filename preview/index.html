How it works

1. Discover

Browse owner-posted listings and evaluate details yourself.

Send an inquiry only after both parties' verified acceptance is supported. An inquiry is not a reservation.

Owner and renter responsibilities

Owners must have the right to list and are responsible for accuracy, condition, compliance and appropriate insurance. Renters independently verify ownership, safety, qualifications and coverage. Both parties agree on all financial and damage terms directly. KAVOLTRI cannot guarantee a liability waiver and legal obligations may apply.
